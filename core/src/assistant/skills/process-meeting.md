# Carrying a meeting into the notes

You have just read the meeting and saved its summary. Now keep what lasts from it in the notes, the way the people-profile skill says for people.

1. List what the meeting was about: the people who spoke or were named (with what they do, what they said they want, what they promised), the projects and the topics, the decisions and the dates.
2. For each, find its note: the index first, then brain_search by name and alias. Read it with brain_read before changing it.
3. Write it down:
   - something that happened or was decided: brain_append a line to the note's timeline;
   - what is true now changed (a new role, a new deadline, a project's new state): brain_write the note with the new state, the old one moved to the timeline;
   - a person or a project with no note yet: create it only as the people-profile skill says (one mention in passing is not enough).
4. Every line says where it comes from: `[Source: meeting:<id>#<n> | YYYY-MM-DD]`, with `<n>` the transcript line, so it can be checked. Quote the words when the wording matters.

What does not go in the notes: the summary itself (it stays on the meeting's page), small talk, one-off errands, anything about health, money or intimate matters unless the note is marked private, ID and account numbers. If the meeting brought nothing lasting, change nothing.

End with one short line: which notes you changed.
